Child Safety Standards – Be Bro

Be Bro is committed to ensuring a safe environment for all users.

Our Commitment

We have a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Any content or behavior involving minors that violates this policy will result in immediate action, including account suspension and reporting to authorities.

Safety Measures

  • Users can report inappropriate behavior directly within the app.
  • We actively monitor and review reports.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement when required.
