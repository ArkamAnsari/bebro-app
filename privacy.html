Privacy Policy for Be Bro

Effective Date: April 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Be Bro ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our mobile application (the "App"). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the App, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products, or otherwise contact us. The personal information that we collect depends on the context of your interactions with the App.

  • A. Personal Information Provided by You: Account Data: Name, username, email address, date of birth, and passwords. Profile Data: Avatars, bios, gaming platform preferences, play styles, and game libraries. Authentication Data: We utilize Google Sign-In. We collect the profile information provided by your Google account (such as your name and email) upon your authorized login.
  • B. Location Data: To facilitate our "Radar" and matchmaking features, we may request access or permission to track location-based information from your mobile device, either continuously or while you are using the App. You can change our access or permissions at any time in your device's settings.
  • C. User-Generated Content: We collect data regarding your interactions within the App, including chat messages, SquadHub posts, friend connections ("Yaars"), and user reports submitted for moderation purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

Having accurate information about you permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we may use information collected about you via the App to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Facilitate matchmaking and display your profile on the local player Radar.
  • Enable user-to-user communications and voice chat features.
  • Monitor and enforce community guidelines (including managing user reports, warnings, and bans).
  • Deliver targeted updates and system notifications.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We may share your information with third parties that perform services for us or on our behalf. We utilize the following Google/Firebase services:

  • Firebase Authentication: For secure user login.
  • Cloud Firestore: For secure database storage of profiles and messages.
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Performance: To monitor app stability and fix bugs.
  • Google Play Services: For core Android functionality and location services.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data at any time.

Account Purge: We provide a fully automated "Delete Account" feature within the App settings. Initiating this process will immediately and permanently erase your authentication records, public profile, private profile, and free up your username.

6. Security of Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information, leveraging Google's Firebase infrastructure. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us, please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable.
